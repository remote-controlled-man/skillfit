# Independent debugging followup declaration

The [first completed study](../../evidence/2026-10-14-debugging-independent-validation.md)
retains no observed correctness regression and a 16.82% uncached-input reduction
point estimate. Its upper task-bootstrap bound does not establish at least 10%,
so its frozen decision remains inconclusive and ineligible. It ends at 125 actual
executor invocations, within its 128-call ceiling. No trials are added to it.

The followup broadens the task mix using eight different historical defects in
three new repositories: p-debounce, debounce and humanize-duration. The original
and complete compact v4 resources stay unchanged. Selection uses public
functional defects and deterministic original/maintainer integrity checks;
no followup model outcomes exist at selection. Existing results are not pooled
into this study, and neither candidate nor threshold is changed to obtain a pass.

## Fixed input and spending

Freeze the [complete task set](../../benches/contrib/oss-skill-followup/README.md),
outcome graders, original/v4 resources, implementation, recorded configuration,
five trials per task, balanced adjacent order and 1200-second deadlines before
the first executor call. The [request example](../examples/eval-request.diagnosing-v4-followup-efficiency.json)
has those settings. An execution-specific declaration binds its absolute request,
preview, input and runtime hashes before launch; the example itself spends nothing.

This is one fixed followup of at most **80 executor calls**, with no readiness,
generator or judge calls. Combined with the sealed first study, cumulative
executor spending is capped at **205 calls**. All previous failed and partial
usage remains in separate ledgers and cumulative spending, never as quality
observations. Authoring, provider-internal requests and dollar billing remain
unknown where the available counters do not measure them.

Stop on an executor, configuration or timing error. Functional failures are
observations and do not permit sample replacement. Do not retry a failed call,
append trials or change a task, grader, candidate or policy within the cohort.
Require AC and verified temporary idle/system sleep assertions before launch;
retain the sleep-gap guard and release assertions on termination.

## Unchanged acceptance and delivery

Keep the explicit version-2 efficiency policy: eight tasks with five pairs each,
complete final all-invocation usage, verified native receipts, declared validation,
balanced order, no observed pair/task pass, score or check regression, at least
75% of tasks reducing uncached input per success, and a 95% task-bootstrap upper
bound supporting at least 10% reduction. Use 10,000 whole-task resamples and seed
20261013. Allow no input, cached/uncached input or output per-success increase
in the declared aggregate; elapsed increase is capped at 25%. Individual task
cost increases remain visible. Preserve the original quality verdict separately;
an all-pass tie does not establish equivalence or a correctness improvement.

Any eligible result is advisory on this task mix and recorded environment. The
component never upgrades automatically. Previously observed normalize-url and
LRU lifecycle cost increases remain limits on generalization. Independent
repositories and defects do not establish benefit for every debugging workload
or future model. Publish the full fixed outcome even if it is again inconclusive
or negative. No further study is silently substituted into this declaration.

Reproduce all completed grades, separate cost populations, confidence intervals
and cumulative spending offline without model calls. Preserve all published
evidence byte-for-byte. Finish source/package checks, mirrored documentation and
verified main-branch integration only with honest, scoped efficacy conclusions.
The original goal remains active until its full completion audit passes.
